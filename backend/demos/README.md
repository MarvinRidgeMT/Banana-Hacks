Put the demo photos here, named as in demos.json:

    hero.png        hero_mask.png        hero_faces.json (optional)
    inferred.png    inferred_mask.png
    light.png       light_mask.png
    backup.png      backup_mask.png

Masks: white where the original content is lost (tears, missing emulsion,
deep scratches), black everywhere else, same size as the photo. Fading and
stains stay black.

Fill in credit, source_url and license in demos.json for every photo.
